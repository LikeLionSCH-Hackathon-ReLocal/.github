## Re:Local — 밭 볼텼유?

**Re:Local** is a team hackathon project exploring trust-based farmland matching between older landowners and young aspiring farmers. Created for the **2025 LIKELION hackathon**, it reframes local economic revitalization through better use of agricultural resources and more opportunities for new growers.

Its guiding idea is simple: **data helps identify opportunities; trust helps people move forward.**

## What we designed

<p align="center"><img src="./assets/matching-flow.svg" width="100%" alt="Matching flow: farmland listing, trust profile, matching, communication" /></p>

- **Trust-aware profiles** bringing together agricultural training, qualifications, equipment, and experience.
- **AI-assisted matching** that combines crop-oriented K-Means clustering with rule-based scoring of suitability factors such as location, price, and facilities.
- **Two user journeys:** map-based discovery for younger users and simpler, step-by-step listing interactions for older landowners.
- **Mutual-consent communication** designed to support post-match conversations.

These are project implementation and design descriptions, **not independently established performance metrics or a claim of an active production service**.
